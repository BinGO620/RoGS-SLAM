## 0. Headline

Two findings, and the second matters more than the first.

1. **The pose-controlled screen works.** On the control arm the headline metric collapsed to a
   seed-to-seed sd of **0.0123 dB — 0.04× the self-tracked null**. The pose channel was carrying
   ~96% of the noise, exactly as amendment #01 §B argued.
2. **With that noise gone, the claim fails.** D improves the 16% non-vacated periphery by
   **+1.59 dB (3.2× its own sd, all 3 seeds)** and the 66% vacated region — the region the paper
   is about — by **+0.22 dB (1.0× its own sd, sign flips on seed 2)**. The effect is real and
   concentrated in the wrong place.

## 2. Q2 — does carve arm over 439 frames? **Yes, but marginally and unstably**

## 3. Q1 — noise collapse: the screen works, and the treatment is now the noise

On `static_vacated_psnr` (amendment #01 headline), against the 7-replicate self-tracked null
sd of 0.278 dB:

| arm | seed values | sd | ratio vs self-tracked null |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B_deferred** (control) | 14.5165 / 14.4953 / 14.4952 | **0.0123** | **0.04×** |
| **D_exit** (treatment) | 14.7962 / 14.9024 / 14.4749 | 0.2226 | 0.80× |

The control arm is **essentially a constant** — three seeds span 0.021 dB. That is the pre-flight's
whole justification confirmed: measured through a 30 cm self-tracked trajectory this same metric
had an sd of 0.278 dB, and virtually all of it was the pose channel.

**But the treatment arm did not collapse (0.80×).** Nothing about the pose channel differs between
the arms — the trajectory is frozen and identical, ATE 2.0618 on all six runs. D's variance is
**generated by its own mechanism**, whose activity varies 10× across seeds (§2).

Other metrics, for the record: `static_vacated_depth_l1_pen_cm` B 0.89× / D 0.55×;
`static_ghost_excess_psnr_db` B **3.23×** (the paired contrast gets *worse* under frozen pose —
see §5); `refined_num_gaussians` B 0.16× / D 0.61×; `ate_rmse_cm` 0.00× both arms by construction.

F1 required D activity PASS **and** |D−B| on `static_vacated_psnr` > per-arm seed sd.

**D seed 2 (14.4749) sits below all three B runs** (min 14.4952). Against a control whose entire
seed spread is 0.021 dB, that flip is not measurement noise — it is the treatment being unstable.

prereg §6 binds this: "GO 需 ≥2 seed 同向，禁止单 seed 宣胜" was written after the balloon
seed-flip burned this project once. A margin equal to 1.00× the treatment arm's own sd, with the
sign reversing on one of three seeds, is inside the noise band. **F2.**

## 5. The finding that outranks the fork gate: the effect is in the wrong region

| region | share of support | B mean | D mean | Δ | Δ / D's own sd | sign across seeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **vacated** (the claim) | 66% of image | 14.5023 | 14.7245 | **+0.2222** | **1.00×** | 2/3, flips on s2 |
| **non-vacated** (periphery) | 16% of image | 16.5840 | 18.1746 | **+1.5907** | **3.17×** | 3/3 consistent |

α-exit **reliably improves the map, and reliably improves it least in the vacated region** — 7×
less than in the periphery, and there not reliably at all. The pre-registered hypothesis (H1a:
α-exit's 出侧 attacks the vacated-region ghost deficit) predicts the opposite ordering.

This is not a noise problem and cannot be fixed by more seeds. It is visible *because* the
instrument finally resolves: the control arm is a constant to 0.02 dB, so there is no room left
to attribute the null to measurement.

It also explains the paired contrast's behaviour a fourth time. `ghost_excess = vacated −
nonvacated` reports D as **worse by 1.37 dB** purely because D's periphery improves more than its
vacated region. Amendment #01 demoted that metric pre-data on exactly this reasoning; the demotion
was correct, and had it not been made this report would have led with "α-exit hurts the headline
by 1.4 dB", which is false.

## 7. Free calibration for the §6 fallback (amendment #01 §D)

The compactness corollary had never been noise-calibrated under controlled poses. From these runs:

- **arm B**: `refined_num_gaussians` sd **878 on a mean of 11296 = CV 7.8%** (vs the self-tracked
  null sd of 5577). Under frozen poses the compactness metric **is resolvable** — a −57%-class
  effect sits far outside a 7.8% band.
- **arm D**: sd 3427, CV 39% — α-exit destroys that stability.

So if the campaign goes to the §6 fallback, the compactness claim is measurable, but it must be
measured on the **A-vs-B** contrast (which this pre-flight did not run) and **without** α-exit.

## 8. What F2 authorises, and one honest caveat about it

prereg §6 CHECKPOINT-1 authorises "先修 exit（carve/reset 阈值/信号）"; amendment #01 §C caps that
at **one round**, with the 08-04 date gate behind it. Available levers: `tau_carve` 0.20 (carve
touches only 7–75 Gaussians), `ema_beta` 0.9 (slow against ~18 lifecycle steps), `reset_value`,
`min_obs_count` 3.

**The caveat:** §5 argues threshold tuning is unlikely to help. Making exit more aggressive removes
more Gaussians *everywhere*, and the present configuration already helps the periphery 7× more than
the vacated region. There is no mechanism by which a lower `tau_carve` would invert that ratio. The
lever that would is a *spatially targeted* exit — which is new design, not a threshold, and does not
fit inside 19 days with writing starting ~08-06.

Recorded as an argument, not a decision. Per prereg §9 and amendment #01 §C, the call is the user's.
